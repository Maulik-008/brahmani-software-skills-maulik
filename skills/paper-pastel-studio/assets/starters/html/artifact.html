faq

Questions, answered

Can I change the accent colour?

Yes. Change one hue in the tokens. The accent is for links and focus only, never a button fill.

Does it work in dark mode?

Yes. Every colour is a token with a dark value, so no component needs dark-specific styling.

Why only six colours?

Colour codes categories. Six is enough to tell things apart and few enough to stay calm.
